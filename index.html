The Man

Rooted in Ilorin. Built for service.

Engr. Abdulquawiy Abdulganiyu Olododo is a proud son of Ibagun Ward, Ilorin East. He holds a degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Kwara State University and an MBA from the University of East London, and is COREN-certified.

He started in enterprise, then carried that drive into public service across agriculture, social investment, solid minerals and infrastructure. Now he is bringing that record of delivery to the National Assembly.

"You will always be able to check me."

Enterprise

Social Investment

Commissioner for Works
