01 / The story

A career built across very different rooms.

From youth leadership and agribusiness to agriculture, social investment and infrastructure, Olododo's public journey has moved through several parts of Kwara's development story.

“Everything I will do in Abuja will orbit two commitments.” Local economic opportunity and transparent, accountable representation are the two commitments stated at his April 2026 declaration.
02 / The record

From early civic leadership to public administration.

A visual timeline of the roles and areas of work that define the public record presented on this site.

2005–2011
Children's Parliament

Leadership roles in the Kwara State Children's Parliament and National Children's Parliament formed an early part of his civic experience.

2010–2013
KIDSEF

The Kiddies Sight-Saving Endowment Fund is cited as supporting corrective eye surgeries for more than 170 children during this period.

2016–2020
Agribusiness

Co-founded Heart & Capital, an agribusiness enterprise associated with youth training, farm management, mechanised farming and agricultural enterprise.

2020–2022
Agriculture

Served as Technical Assistant on Agriculture to Governor AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq, working within the state's agriculture transformation efforts.

2022–2023
KWASSIP

Served as General Manager of the Kwara State Social Investment Programme, which focuses on social protection, economic empowerment and support for vulnerable groups.

2023–2026
Kwara Executive Council

Served in the Kwara State Executive Council, including the Solid Minerals and Works portfolios, with responsibilities spanning infrastructure and public works.

2026
House of Representatives bid

Declared his intention to contest the Ilorin East / Ilorin South Federal Constituency seat in the 2027 general election and subsequently appears on the APC candidate platform.

03 / Infrastructure

The works chapter.

His Works portfolio placed him close to road construction, bridges, flyovers, rural access and other public infrastructure projects across Kwara.

Projects named in published profiles Dr. Ibrahim Sulu-Gambari Unity Flyover, General Tunde Idiagbon Flyover, Wahab Folawiyo Road and the Ajase-Ipo–Oke-Iya Road rehabilitation are among projects associated with the period.
04 / Social investment

The KWASSIP chapter.

KWASSIP was established in 2019 with a focus on social inclusion, poverty reduction, economic empowerment and support for vulnerable households. Olododo served as its General Manager from 2022 to 2023.

05 / Declared agenda

What he says he will take to Abuja.

The following is a structured presentation of the commitments stated publicly at his April 2026 declaration. It is presented as a record of the agenda, not as an assessment of its likely results.

01

Build a functioning local economy

Create pathways to income and economic opportunity for traders, farmers and young people in the constituency.

02

Connect people to federal finance

Connect entrepreneurs with intervention and financing opportunities associated with BOI, NIRSAL and CBN small and medium enterprise facilities.

03

Technical and digital skills

Train young people in technical trades, digital skills and the remote-work economy.

04

Transparent representation

Make constituency representation transparent and accountable, with public visibility into activities and performance.
